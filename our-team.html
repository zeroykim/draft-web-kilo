Wayan
Head Chef
As the creative force behind our culinary delights, our chef has a passion for blending diverse flavors and techniques from around the world. With a background in South American cuisine, he has honed his skills in Europe before finding his culinary home in Bali. His dishes are a testament to his innovative spirit, fusing traditional recipes with modern twists to create a truly unique dining experience. From vibrant ceviches to hearty European classics, each dish is a celebration of global flavors that will transport you on a culinary journey like no other.

With her extensive knowledge of spirits, liqueurs, and fresh ingredients, she has mastered the art of mixology, creating cocktails that are not only visually stunning but also a treat for the palate. Whether it’s a classic cocktail or a bespoke creation, each drink is meticulously crafted to perfection, ensuring a memorable experience for our guests.
