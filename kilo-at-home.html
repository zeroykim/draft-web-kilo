Events & Catering by Bespoke

Bespoke is the newest addition to Kilo Group services in response to the increasing demand for the outlets to cater for special occasions and events anywhere by customer request. Bespoke provides the best selections of many different styles of Asian Fusion Cuisine for you to choose, bar service and customize your needs for your special occasion. Putting all of our hearts in everything we do, Kilo values creativity and sincerity to ensure the satisfaction of the guest.

Ready to bring your vision to life with Bespoke?

1
Provide Event Details
Share key event information: number of guests, event date, duration, and location.
2
Choose Menu Options
Select items from our canapé or full-seating menu, along with drink options. We’ll then create a customized package and send an initial quotation.
3
Review and Adjust
Review the proposal, and let us know if adjustments are needed for dietary restrictions or personal preferences. We’ll provide a final quotation after any changes.
4
Secure Your Booking with a Deposit
Finalize the details with a deposit payment, and we’ll take care of the rest!
