Private Space

Indulge in an Exclusive Culinary Experience in our private space now! Located in Seminyak-Bali, you will find the Kendra Art Space on the small and quiet Drupadi Street. Set in a leafy garden with a manicured lawn with lovely outdoor space next to the gallery Opened in 2008. Its mission is to promote contemporary art and design by Indonesian and International artists.


We understand that special moments are meant to be celebrated in a unique and intimate setting. This unique modern minimalist space is now available whether you’re hosting a birthday celebration, corporate event, or a family reunion, our private space can be customized to suit your specific needs. From personalized menus curated by our expert chefs to bespoke decor that aligns with your event theme, we’re committed to making your vision a reality.


Our dedicated space provides an exclusive retreat for you and your guests, allowing for an intimate dining experience away from the hustle and bustle of the main restaurant and you can also complete your stay with Uma Sapna Villa, just minutes away from Kilo Seminyak, our villa provides easy access to the best that Seminyak has to offer.
